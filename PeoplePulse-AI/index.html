THE IDEA

Small signals.
Meaningful change.

Many public challenges are easy to notice, yet hard to understand as a whole. PeoplePulse AI gives communities a structured way to describe them.

01 / THE CHALLENGE
◌

Problems are felt unevenly.

Essential service access can vary by street. Without organized feedback, those differences are difficult to see clearly.

02 / OUR APPROACH
✳

Listen with structure.

Category-specific surveys turn real experiences into consistent responses that can be compared and understood.

03 / THE POSSIBILITY
↗

See the bigger picture.

Python analysis and Power BI visualization can reveal patterns across categories and locations.

KEY FEATURES

Feedback, made
useful for insight.

Each response is collected in a consistent format that can support future analysis while keeping the survey focused for participants.

01 / FOCUSED
✳

One topic at a time.

Each participant answers only 15 questions for the category they select.

02 / STRUCTURED
▤

Comparable responses.

Clear, measurable answer choices help organize community feedback.

03 / LOCATION-AWARE
⌖

Understand local patterns.

Location fields help compare reported needs by district and area.
